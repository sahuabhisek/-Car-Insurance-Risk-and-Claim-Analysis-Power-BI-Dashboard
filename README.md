# -Car-Insurance-Risk-and-Claim-Analysis-Power-BI-Dashboard# 🚗 Car Insurance Risk and Claim Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Car Insurance Risk and Claim Analysis** project is an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The primary objective of this project is to analyze car insurance claim amounts across different customer demographics, vehicle manufacturers, vehicle usage categories, coverage zones and other business dimensions.

The dashboard transforms insurance data into meaningful visual insights and provides an interactive environment for exploring claim distribution.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the overall insurance claim amount.
- Monitor the total number of insurance policies.
- Understand average claim frequency and average claim amount.
- Compare Private and Commercial vehicle claims.
- Analyze claim amounts by car manufacturer.
- Explore geographical coverage-zone distributions.
- Understand claim patterns across customer age groups.
- Examine historical claim distribution by car year.
- Analyze claim amounts by Kids Driving categories.
- Compare claim distributions across education levels.
- Explore education and marital status together.

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Total Claim Amount by Car Use
Analyzes the distribution of insurance claim amounts between:
- Private
- Commercial

This visualization helps compare claims across vehicle usage categories.

### 2. Total Claim Amount by Car Make
Compares claim amounts across different car manufacturers:
- Ford
- Chevrolet
- Dodge
- Toyota
- GMC

The displayed chart places Ford at the top of the comparison.

### 3. Total Claim Amount by Coverage Zone
Analyzes claim distribution across:
- Urban
- Highly Urban
- Rural
- Suburban
- Highly Rural

This helps explore geographical differences in insurance claims.

### 4. Total Claim Amount by Age Group
Examines insurance claim distribution across different age groups:
- 15–25
- 26–35
- 36–45
- 46–55
- 56–65
- 66–75

This analysis helps understand claim distribution across customer age segments.

### 5. Total Claim Amount by Car Year
Displays historical claim distribution according to vehicle manufacturing year.

This helps explore how claim amounts vary across different vehicle-year categories.

### 6. Total Claim Amount by Kids Driving
Examines the distribution of claim amounts across Kids Driving categories.

### 7. Total Claim Amount by Education
Analyzes claim amounts across education categories:
- Bachelors
- High School
- Masters
- PhD

### 8. Education vs. Marital Status
A Matrix visual compares claim amounts across education and marital status categories:
- Divorced
- Married
- Single

This provides a multi-dimensional view of customer segments.
